Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 6 July 2026

1. About Financial Coach

Financial Coach is a free personal finance tracking Progressive Web App (PWA) designed for Indian Rupee (₹) transactions. The application runs entirely in your browser — there are no Financial Coach servers, no user accounts, and no cloud storage operated by Financial Coach. Your financial data never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable Google Drive backup or use an AI feature.

2. Data We Collect and Store

All data you enter — including transactions, accounts, budgets, goals, categories, merchants, and AI chat conversations — is stored locally in your browser using SQLite WASM (sql.js) and IndexedDB. Financial Coach does not operate any servers and never receives, transmits, or stores your financial data on its own infrastructure.

We do not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, or any form of telemetry. No data is collected about how you use the application.

3. Google OAuth Permissions

If you choose to connect your Google account (for Gmail sync or Google Drive backup), we request the following OAuth scopes. These are requested only when you initiate the connection in Settings.

  • gmail.readonly (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly): Read your Gmail messages to extract bank and payment transaction details. Only email bodies from sender addresses you specify are processed. Emails are read on your device and are never transmitted to or stored on any Financial Coach server.
  • drive.appdata (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata): Read and write a single encrypted backup file in the hidden appdata folder of your Google Drive. This folder is not visible in your normal Drive file list. The backup file is encrypted with AES-GCM using a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2) before it is uploaded, so Google cannot read its contents and neither can Financial Coach.
  • openid and email: Identify your Google account so that your email address can be displayed in the Settings screen and used to authenticate access to your Drive appdata folder.

OAuth tokens are stored only inside the in-browser Credential Vault after you set a PIN and unlock it. If you lose your device or forget your PIN, revoke Google access via your Google Account permissions page , reconnect Gmail from Settings, and restore your latest encrypted Drive backup if needed.

Financial Coach's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

4. AI Providers

Financial Coach supports optional AI features (chat assistant and Gmail transaction extraction). You may configure your own API keys for one of the following providers: Groq, OpenAI (GPT), Google Gemini, Azure OpenAI, or Ollama (local). API keys are stored only inside the in-browser Credential Vault after you set a PIN and unlock it, and they are never sent to Financial Coach servers.

When you use an AI feature, a prompt containing relevant financial context is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you configured. External providers (Groq, OpenAI, Gemini, Azure OpenAI) are used only after you explicitly consent in the app; Ollama runs locally on your device and does not require external sharing. Before sending, our maskPII() function masks detected identifiers such as phone numbers, email addresses, PAN, Aadhaar, UPI handles, and labelled account / card references when possible. Account names are replaced with anonymous references (for example, 'Account 1') before sending, so manually renamed account names are never included in AI prompts. Financial Coach does not intercept, log, or store AI prompts on its own servers, but your configured provider processes requests under its own privacy policy, retention, and region settings.

AI prompts may include masked transaction descriptions, anonymised account summaries, budgets, goals, and Gmail-derived content relevant to the feature you triggered (for example chat or Gmail extraction). Goal names and budget names you create are sent as typed — avoid including personal names or sensitive details in those fields if you use an external AI provider. Free-form messages you type in the AI chat are processed by maskPII(), but masking is pattern-based and may not catch every personal name or custom phrase you type. Do not include secrets, real names, or personal details in goal names, budget names, or free-form chat messages unless you are comfortable sharing them with your chosen provider.

You can withdraw external AI consent at any time from Settings. After revocation, chat and Gmail extraction remain available in local / heuristic mode until you opt in again.

5. Session Security

By default, all locally stored data is automatically wiped after 6 hours of browser inactivity. This protects your data if you forget to close the app on a shared or public device. The inactivity timer is reset whenever you interact with the application (click, scroll, type).

You can opt in to Trusted Device mode (Settings → Session Security) to disable automatic wiping on a device you own and trust. This setting is stored in localStorage and applies only to the current browser profile.

6. Google Drive Encrypted Backup

If you enable Google Drive sync, your Financial Coach database is serialised and encrypted entirely in your browser before being uploaded. Encryption uses AES-GCM (256-bit) with a key derived from your chosen passphrase using PBKDF2 (SHA-256, 100,000 iterations). Financial Coach never has access to your passphrase or the plaintext database content. The encrypted file is stored in your Drive appdata folder, which is accessible only to this application.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times. You can:

  • Export your database as a binary backup file (.db) via Settings → Export Data.
  • Export transactions as a CSV or PDF file via Settings → Export CSV / Export PDF.
  • Import a previously exported binary backup to restore your data via Settings → Import Backup.
  • Delete the Google Drive backup file via Settings → Google Drive → Delete Backup.

Financial Coach does not have a manual data-wipe button. To permanently delete all locally stored data, you must clear this site's stored data in your browser settings. Note that clearing browser history or the browser cache alone does not erase app data — you must specifically clear site data or cookies and site data for this domain (e.g., in Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Site Settings → View permissions and data stored across sites; in Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Manage Data).

The automatic session wipe (6 hours of inactivity by default — see Section 5) also permanently erases all local data and credentials.

8. Your Rights

Under the Information Technology Act, 2000 (India) and applicable data protection principles, you have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal data. Because all data is stored locally in your browser, you exercise these rights directly within the application — there is no need to contact us to access or delete your data.

If you have questions or concerns about how Google processes data obtained through OAuth, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy .

9. Third-Party Services

Financial Coach does not share your data with any third parties beyond the services you explicitly configure (Google OAuth, your chosen AI provider). Each service operates under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Groq Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini API Terms

Financial Coach is deployed on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may collect anonymised network traffic metadata (e.g., IP addresses, request logs) as part of its standard CDN and security operations. Please refer to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy for details.

10. Children's Privacy

Financial Coach is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the application and you have concerns, please contact us at the address below.
